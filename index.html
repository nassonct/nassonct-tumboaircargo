Simple shipping process

How to Ship with Tumbo Air Cargo

  1. 01

    Get Your Warehouse Address

    Contact us for the official China or Dubai warehouse address and your unique customer shipping mark.

  2. 02

    Send Goods to Our Warehouse

    Buy from your preferred supplier and ask them to deliver eligible goods using your assigned shipping mark.

  3. 03

    Cargo Transportation & Handling

    We coordinate receipt, processing, air transportation, and relevant customs clearance procedures.

  4. 04

    Collect Your Cargo in Dar es Salaam

    Collect from our Kariakoo office or ask whether local delivery is available for your shipment.

Before you ship

Know What You Can Ship

Cargo acceptance depends on the item, route, airline rules, and required documentation. Ask us to confirm before purchasing or dispatching goods.

Standard Cargo

  • General merchandise
  • Clothing and fashion products
  • Shoes and accessories
  • Household products
  • Other approved general goods

Special Cargo — Check Before Shipping

  • Lithium batteries and electronics containing batteries
  • Liquids and powders
  • Cosmetics and perfumes
  • Other restricted or regulated goods

Special cargo may need additional handling, approval, or documentation. These items are not automatically accepted.
