## Brand & Style

Tumbo Air Cargo's visual identity is clear, dependable, and welcoming. The site
serves individuals and businesses shipping goods from China and Dubai to Dar es
Salaam. Favor practical service information and clear next steps over
unsupported performance claims or simulated shipment data.

## Colors

- **Navy (`#0B1F3A`):** Headers, footer, and high-priority text.
- **Blue (`#1266E8`):** Primary actions, links, focus states, and interactive
  accents.
- **Orange (`#F28C28`):** Small emphasis details and notices; do not use it as
  the sole signal for important status.
- **Light canvas (`#F5F8FC`):** Section backgrounds and supporting panels.
- **White (`#FFFFFF`):** Cards, forms, and primary content surfaces.
- **Body slate (`#334155`):** Reading text.
- **Muted slate (`#64748B`):** Secondary information.
- **Border slate (`#E2E8F0`):** Dividers and control boundaries.

## Typography

Use the locally hosted Manrope font throughout the site. Keep headings
short and strongly weighted; body copy should be readable and comfortably
spaced. Use uppercase, letter-spaced text only for brief section labels.

## Layout & Spacing

Use the shared `max-w-7xl` container with responsive horizontal gutters.
Sections should stack naturally on small screens and use two- or three-column
grids only when the content benefits from comparison. Preserve clear spacing
between sections and avoid dense dashboard-style layouts.

## Components

- **Primary button:** Blue background, white semibold text, rounded corners,
  visible hover and keyboard-focus states.
- **Secondary button:** White or transparent background, blue border and text.
- **Cards:** White surface, subtle slate border, rounded corners, and restrained
  shadow where elevation improves grouping.
- **Forms:** Explicit labels, native validation, useful keyboard focus, and
  clear optional-field labels.
- **Notices:** Use a light background and a distinct border. State clearly when
  online tracking, rates, or warehouse addresses are not available.
- **Navigation:** Keep desktop links visible at wide breakpoints and provide an
  accessible toggle for smaller screens.
- **Language control:** Keep English and Swahili choices available on every
  page, indicate the active choice, and localize accessible labels.

## Content & Interaction

- Describe the supported China/Dubai to Dar es Salaam routes and the services
  actually offered.
- Do not invent rates, tracking outcomes, warehouse addresses, certifications,
  statistics, or exact map coordinates.
- Forms open a prefilled WhatsApp message for the visitor to review and send;
  they do not imply backend submission.
- Until a tracking integration exists, say so plainly and direct visitors to
  WhatsApp assistance.
- Confirm special-cargo acceptance with the team before promising transport.
- Use the supplied Kariakoo location and phone numbers; link to a location-based
  map search rather than implying precise coordinates.
